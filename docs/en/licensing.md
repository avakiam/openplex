## The core: AGPL-3.0

The OpenPlex core (this repository) is free software under the **GNU Affero
General Public License, version 3 only**.

- **Using it** — for yourself, your company or your customers, commercially
  or not — is free and needs no permission.
- **Offering it unmodified**, for example as a server image at a hosting
  provider, is allowed; you must keep the licence notices and make the
  source available (a link to this repository is enough).
- **Modifying it** is allowed. If you let anyone else use your modified
  version — by giving them a copy **or over a network**, as a hosting
  provider does — you must publish your changes' source code under the
  AGPL-3.0, and you can't call it "OpenPlex" (see below).

## Extensions: any licence, also paid

The [OpenPlex Extension Exception](../../LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md) is an extra
permission on top of the AGPL: an **extension** that works with OpenPlex only
through its documented extension interface (today, the HTTP API; later also
the extension manifest, hooks and dashboard slots) may use **any licence**,
including closed source and paid, even when installed and run together with
OpenPlex.

- You can build and sell your own extensions, or give them away.
- An extension that copies OpenPlex source code is, for that part, under the
  AGPL.
- Modifying OpenPlex itself is still covered by the AGPL.

## Official extensions

The OpenPlex project will publish official extensions:

- **free and open source** ones, in the public extensions repository;
- **paid** ones, bought once and licensed under their own terms (number of
  servers, updates and support are set out in each extension's licence).

## Trademark

The code is free; the **name and logos are not**. You may say your service
"uses" or "is compatible with" OpenPlex and offer unmodified releases under
the name. A modified version must use another name, and an extension can't
present itself as official unless it is. Details in
[`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md).

## Contributions

Code contributions aren't accepted yet, so the project keeps full ownership
of the code. When they open, contributors will sign a Contributor License
Agreement. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
